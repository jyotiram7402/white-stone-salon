Privacy

This website keeps nothing about you. This page says exactly what happens when you use it.

No server, nothing stored

The site is a set of plain files. It has no server of its own, no database and no accounts, and it stores nothing about the people who visit it.

The booking form

What you type into the booking form is kept as a draft in your own browser, in the tab you are using, so that it is still there if the page reloads. It stays in your browser until you send it through WhatsApp.

Pressing “Send request on WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with your request written out. It is sent only when you press send there. From then on it is a WhatsApp message between you and the salon.

The same browser tab also remembers that the opening animation has played, so that it does not play twice.

Fonts and the animation libraries

The typefaces load from Google Fonts. The two libraries that animate the page and smooth its scrolling load from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to those two services to fetch the files.

No cookies, no analytics

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
