# Sample photos and their credits

The 21 photos now on the site are **samples from Unsplash**. They are free to use, including commercially, under the Unsplash Licence (https://unsplash.com/license), and no credit is required. The photographers are listed here anyway so every photo can be traced.

Read this before launch:

- These photos show **other people and other salons**. They stand in for the salon's own photos so the design can be judged. Replace them before the site goes live (see `docs/PHOTOS.md`).
- The licence covers the photo, not the people in it. Using a stranger's face to advertise a real business is the kind of use a photographer or model can object to. That is one more reason to replace them.
- No sample is used for the **hair artists** or the **shopfront**. A stranger must never be shown as a member of staff, and a different shop must never be shown as this one.
- Only free Unsplash photos were chosen. Nothing from Unsplash+ (the paid collection) is used.
- Each photo was opened and looked at on 11 November 2026. If one is later removed from Unsplash, its frame falls back to the stone surface and "Photo needed".

All addresses below begin with `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-`. They are set in `data/photos.js`.
